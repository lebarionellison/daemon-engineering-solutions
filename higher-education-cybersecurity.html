BUILT AROUND UNIVERSITY ENVIRONMENTS

Security must account for the whole research and campus ecosystem.

Our assessment conversations are designed for central IT, information security, research computing, infrastructure, academic technology and research security leaders. Final scope is determined with the institution and its system owners.

Identity & access

Student, faculty, staff, administrator, researcher, service and privileged identities; MFA coverage, access lifecycle, permissions and authentication controls.

Research computing & HPC

Compute clusters, GPU environments, schedulers, research workflows, shared systems, data movement and workload isolation.

Cloud & campus systems

Cloud services, collaboration platforms, learning systems, web applications, APIs, networks and remote access.

Research data & storage

Storage permissions, sensitive research data, data sharing, backup architecture, retention and recovery pathways.

Ransomware resilience

Backup isolation, privileged access to recovery systems, restoration exercises, incident escalation and operational continuity.

Detection & response readiness

Security telemetry coverage, alert pathways, incident roles, escalation procedures and opportunities to improve visibility.

UNIVERSITY CYBERSECURITY & RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE READINESS ASSESSMENT

A structured starting point—not a checkbox certification.

The initial engagement is designed to establish an agreed picture of critical assets, identity controls, exposure, resilience and improvement priorities. It is not a guarantee that an institution is secure, nor a substitute for a full penetration test, audit or compliance assessment.

01 / DISCOVER

Scope & critical assets

Identify participating teams, systems, data classes, dependencies and authorized review boundaries.

02 / REVIEW

Controls & exposure

Review agreed identity, cloud, network, application, research compute and storage controls.

03 / PRIORITIZE

Risk & attack paths

Organize observations by business/research impact, likelihood, dependencies and remediation effort.

04 / PLAN

Remediation roadmap

Deliver findings, prioritized recommendations, ownership suggestions and verification steps.

POTENTIAL DELIVERABLES · AGREED IN THE STATEMENT OF WORK

What the institution can receive

Executive summary; agreed asset and control coverage; prioritized findings with evidence; identity and access observations; research/HPC/storage considerations; backup and recovery observations; remediation roadmap; risk acceptance items; and a proposed verification plan.

Deliverables vary by scope and evidence available. No sensitive system details need to be submitted through this public website.

AUTHORIZATION, PRIVACY & QUALIFICATIONS

Institutional trust starts before testing begins.

Written authorization

No penetration test, scanning beyond agreed passive review, simulated attack or intrusive validation begins without written authorization from the institution or authorized system owner. Scope, assets, testing windows, rules of engagement, stop conditions and emergency contacts are documented first.

Data handling

Before work begins, the statement of work defines approved access methods, minimum necessary data, transfer and storage locations, authorized tools and subprocessors, confidentiality, retention/deletion timelines and incident notification contacts. Client data is not placed into unapproved tools or used to train public AI models.

Qualifications & procurement

Proposed personnel, relevant experience, verifiable certifications (if held), subcontractors, insurance evidence and references (where available and authorized) are disclosed during qualification and procurement. DAEMON does not imply university approval, accreditation, certifications or credentials that have not been verified.

The final engagement must be accepted only where appropriately qualified personnel, tools, insurance and contractual requirements can be met. Regulatory interpretations and compliance attestations remain with the institution's designated legal, privacy and compliance professionals.
