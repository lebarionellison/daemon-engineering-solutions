02 / PRODUCT CAPABILITIES

ONE WORKFLOW FROM MODEL TO MANAGED DEVICE.

ACE Edge Intelligence is the embedded deployment layer inside DAEMON ACE. This release delivers the profile, preflight and artifact workflow; hardware execution and fleet operations are staged integrations.

01 · PROFILEDevice & runtime profiles

Capture compute family, OS, accelerator, memory, power and ROS 2 context.

02 · PACKAGEModel deployment planning

Map model formats and edge targets to candidate inference runtimes.

03 · VALIDATEEngineering preflight

Check profile completeness, resource budgets and integration assumptions.

04 · EVIDENCEValidation artifacts

Export structured deployment profiles and machine-readable evidence reports.

05 · OBSERVEEdge telemetry · next milestone

Planned device agent for temperature, power, memory, accelerator and inference telemetry.

06 · MANAGEFleet operations · next milestone

Planned signed updates, staged rollouts, rollback and device health management.
