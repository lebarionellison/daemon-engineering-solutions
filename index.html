DAEMON ACE: Dual-Layer Filtered Tunnel

A security engineering and delivery-assurance concept designed to put policy checks, bounded execution paths and human authorization around AI-assisted infrastructure change.

  • Layer 1: inspect supported prompts, artifacts and requests for configured risk signals.
  • Verified tunnel: define identity, authorization, provenance and egress boundaries for eligible workflows.
  • Layer 2: review supported configuration findings and proposed changes; sensitive production release remains human-authorized.
  • SHA-256 digests can evidence content integrity—not safety, signer identity or approval.
