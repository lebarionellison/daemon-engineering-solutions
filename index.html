Engineering challenges / Real-world failure patterns

Problems We Engineer Solutions For.

When systems scale, hidden dependencies become expensive. We help engineering teams investigate issues affecting throughput, availability, security, and operating cost.

Kubernetes scales—but database pools saturate.

Pod growth multiplies concurrent connections, overwhelming database limits and creating cascading latency or failures.

Cross-region egress drives unexpected bills.

Replication, service traffic, backups, and architecture choices create transfer charges without clear workload attribution.

Multi-region clusters struggle with latency or failover.

Traffic routing, replication lag, regional dependencies, and recovery assumptions undermine resilience.

GPU infrastructure is costly but underutilized.

Scheduling, workload placement, storage I/O, and resource fragmentation can leave expensive accelerators idle.

SRE teams face recurring incidents and alert noise.

Repeated failure patterns, weak observability, and manual response workflows consume engineering capacity.

Security teams lack configuration visibility and response automation.

Drift, access paths, fragmented signals, and manual escalation make consistent response harder.

Infrastructure costs grow faster than application demand.

Overprovisioning, inefficient data movement, licensing, and scaling patterns obscure cost drivers.

Platform teams spend too much time on operational toil.

Manual deployments, repetitive remediation, and inconsistent environments slow delivery.
