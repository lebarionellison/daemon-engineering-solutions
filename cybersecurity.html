Testing begins only after written authorization from the system owner, defined in-scope assets, approved test windows, rules of engagement, emergency contacts and agreed stop conditions.

Data handling & confidentiality

Access is limited to what the engagement requires. Data transfer, storage location, approved tools, retention, deletion and incident notification expectations are documented before work begins.

Before contracting, DAEMON will identify proposed delivery personnel, relevant verifiable experience and credentials, tools, subcontractors and any insurance or procurement evidence applicable to the engagement. No unverified certification or accreditation is implied.
