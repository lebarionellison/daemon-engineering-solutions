A MODULAR ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE

From artifact review to governed delivery.

ACE is organized around four cooperating engineering functions. Scope and integration depth are agreed with each customer rather than implied by a generic feature checklist.

ACE ARCHITECT

Understand the system.

Map workload intent, infrastructure context, trust boundaries, deployment targets and engineering requirements.

ACE BUILDER

Prepare the delivery path.

Generate or refine supported pipeline, configuration and policy artifacts with reviewable outputs.

ACE GUARDIAN

Inspect and challenge.

Apply configured checks, identify selected risks, present remediation proposals and preserve human approval boundaries.

ACE INTELLIGENCE

Learn from operations.

Provide the architectural path for feedback from workload state, runtime telemetry and operational lessons into future engineering decisions.

DAEMON ACE is not represented as a complete AI SOC, SIEM, XDR or MDR platform. The current demo does not connect to live clusters, inspect runtime traffic, enforce network egress, manage secrets, or autonomously contain threats. A Receipt of Safety records integrity of the generated evidence; it is not a certification or guarantee that a workload is safe.
