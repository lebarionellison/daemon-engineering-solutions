OPEN EMBEDDED ECOSYSTEM

ARM & Embedded Linux

Support selected ARM-based edge computers and embedded Linux environments where runtimes, drivers and required interfaces are available.

Hardware names indicate target ecosystems, not universal certification or prebuilt compatibility. Each board, module, camera, accelerator, ROS distribution and runtime combination must be checked against vendor support and tested against customer acceptance criteria. Functional-safety certification is not implied.
