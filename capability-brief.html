RECEIPT OF SAFETY

Release evidence can include artifact hashes, versions, test results, policy decisions, approval identity and time, deployment status, exceptions and rollback references. A receipt is traceability evidence—not a certification or guarantee of safety.

ROBOTICS & PHYSICAL AI

ROS 2 is the central robotics integration layer, with a proposed path for simulation-to-real validation, embedded AI, Jetson / ARM64 packaging, sensor and actuator boundaries, runtime telemetry and controlled release. Software policy does not replace safety-rated controllers, interlocks, emergency stops, guarding or qualified machine-risk assessment.

ENGINEERING DELIVERY

Discovery and architecture → scoped integration → build and validation → controlled pilot → operational handoff. Engagements are defined with customer-approved access, deliverables, acceptance criteria, data handling and rollback expectations.

Current maturity and qualification: DAEMON ACE is in active development. The Jetson Orin Nano ARM64 reference package has passed its CI build and ROS 2 smoke workflow; physical-device acceptance is pending. The public self-healing workbench demonstrates limited deterministic browser-side repair rules, not production AI auto-remediation, a live virtual twin, or deployment. Compatibility across cloud providers, accelerators, industrial controllers and edge boards is qualified per target and integration.
